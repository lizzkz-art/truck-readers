# Truck Readers

A free reading game about big trucks for kindergarten sight words. It runs in any modern browser, works offline after the first load, and keeps everything on the device.

**Play:** https://lizzkz-art.github.io/truck-readers/

## What it teaches
- Dolch pre-primer and primer sight words, and about 35 early Fry words (first 100)
- Letter names and beginning sounds, with truck pictures
- Short CVC words such as bus, cab, dig, mud, van, tug, jet, hog, can, red, big, run, stop, go
- Reading a short sentence and filling in the missing word, spelling with letter tiles, and loading word crates on a trailer

## Design
- 24 levels in four places on a road trip (Minnesota, Maryland, Texas, El Salvador), with a Big Rig Challenge at the end of each place
- Starts with 2 choices and grows to 3 or 4; drops back after misses; stars are always positive
- Win stars to unlock paint colors, stacks, horn sounds, tires, and decals for your own rig
- Every word and sentence is read aloud (recorded voice clips). Truck sounds are made with WebAudio
- Optional Spanish bonus words (camión, grúa, bomberos, rojo, grande, parar)
- Grown-up area behind a PIN: words mastered and words that need practice, set the level, profiles, reset

## Privacy and safety
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
- Nothing is sent anywhere. The only network use is loading the app files
- Progress is saved only in this browser (localStorage)
- Secular, calm content with no scary scenes
